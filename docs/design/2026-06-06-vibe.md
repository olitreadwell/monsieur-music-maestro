# Design vibe: Latin dance club crossed with dance school

## The feeling

Late evening at a dance studio above a salsa club. Warm wood floor, walls of mirrors, a stack of vinyl in the corner, a coffee circle of dancers between sets. Hand-feeling. Embodied. Latin first because that's where the learner is heading (salsa without counting), but the studio teaches the whole world: West Africa, Brazil, India, Iran, the rest. The website is the space the learner walks into.

NOT: developer playground, AI app aesthetic, brutalist landing page, corporate edtech, generic music textbook.

References: Fania Records album sleeves (1970s NYC salsa), Buena Vista Social Club poster, Cuban son LP covers (Egrem, Areito imprints), Brazilian Tropicalia, vintage dance studio interiors, the warm tungsten of a club at 11pm, polished wood floors, conga skins, brass-trim mirrors, a chalkboard of dance steps in white chalk on burgundy.

Replace the current zinc + lime palette wholesale. Lime stays only in the favicon (replace later with the new monogram).

## Typography

Three families, no more.

- **Display**: **Fraunces** (variable serif, italic optical-size variant). The italic feels like Cuban poster lettering and Fania album titles. Used for h1 + h2 on hero areas and lesson-set titles.
- **Body**: **Geist** (already loaded). Used for paragraphs and UI text. Wide character set, clean for ESL/AuDHD readers.
- **Mono**: **Geist Mono** (already loaded). Used for code blocks and the tree ASCII rendering.

H1 in Fraunces semibold, optical-size 144 if available. H2 in Fraunces medium italic, smaller. H3 onwards in Geist semibold.

## Mascot: Em

A persistent friendly learning companion who follows the learner through the journey. Em is the name used in all copy. Em is gender-neutral. Refer to Em as "Em" or "they".

### Persona

A dance teacher with decades of experience across many rhythm traditions. Warm, encouraging, a bit eccentric. Speaks plain language. Sometimes drops a Spanish, Yoruba, or Portuguese word and explains it in the same breath. Never patronising. Never overwhelming. Knows when to be quiet.

Tone in copy: like a teacher you actually want to learn from. "Try this." "Did you notice how that one beat carries the rest?" "Rest a moment. The music will still be here when you come back."

### Visual

Stylised silhouette: a slim figure in comfortable studio clothing, holding either a pair of claves or with one hand raised in a dance lead gesture. Drawn in ~20 to 30 SVG path commands. Colored by `currentColor` so Em inherits context. Sized 16px (footer, inline cue) to 240px (empty states). No facial features beyond two dots for eyes — the silhouette reads as anyone.

Variants (states):
- **`idle`** — standing, claves in hand. Used as a small avatar.
- **`pointing`** — one hand raised toward something. Used to draw attention to a code block or quiz.
- **`clapping`** — both hands together, celebrating. Used on correct-answer or section-complete.
- **`napping`** — head tilted, eyes closed. Used on empty review state ("nothing due, Em is napping").
- **`mid-step`** — caught mid-dance-step. Used in the home tree when the learner is "between" stages.

Each variant is a separate SVG file or a single SVG with `<g>` groups toggled by prop. Choose whichever ships faster.

### Where Em appears

- **Header DropdownNav**: a tiny 16px Em avatar before the "menu" label. Always.
- **Home tree**: Em mid-step sits next to the learner's current spine toy (the next un-read spine item). Walks along as progress advances.
- **Toy page**: Em pointing appears above the quiz / challenge / puzzle, with a one-line hint in Em's voice ("Try changing one thing and replay.").
- **Final review success**: Em clapping, 240px, with copy ("Bien hecho. Onwards.").
- **Empty review state**: Em napping ("Nothing due. Em is resting.").
- **404 page**: Em confused, with copy ("Lost the beat. Try the home page.").
- **Footer**: tiny Em signing off.

### When Em is silent

- The bottom playground bar (Em doesn't interrupt the music tool).
- Inside the Strudel REPL itself.
- During quiz/challenge submission feedback (the feedback is plain, not in Em's voice).

### Optional: Em's notes

A separate UI element. A small folded-paper icon next to certain toys. Click reveals one short note in Em's voice ("This is the toy I always start beginners on. Don't rush.")

Implement as a `<details>` element with `<summary>` for native a11y. Defer to a follow-up slice.

## Stage icons

Each of the 9 stages gets a tiny stroke-based SVG glyph hinting at its content. ~16px to 20px.

| Stage | Glyph |
|---|---|
| 0 — Play | a speaker shape |
| 1 — Pulse | a heart outline |
| 2 — Hats + clap | two clapping hands |
| 3 — Clave | two crossed sticks (the actual instrument) |
| 4 — Counting | three numbered footprints (a salsa basic 1-2-3) |
| 5 — Low end | a conga drum |
| 6 — Colour | a record sleeve circle |
| 7 — Shape | a salsa basic step diagram |
| 8 — Play it out | a turntable |

Tiny. Not loud. Inline with the stage heading.

## Microinteractions

All gated on `motion-safe:` Tailwind variants. Honour reduce-motion.

- **Mark-read**: a tiny burst of three accent-coloured dots that float up like sparks from a conga rim, then fade. ~400ms.
- **Correct answer**: the answer text gives a single small "yes" nod (translateY 2px then back). ~250ms.
- **Tree row hover**: subtle background tint warm-up + 1px horizontal nudge of the prefix glyph (like a dancer's lead).
- **Bottom playground bar open**: spring-easing slide-up. `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` for a tiny overshoot, like a step that lands and settles.

## Texture

Subtle paper grain on the page background (light mode) via a thin SVG noise overlay at 5% opacity. NOT chunky. Just enough that the page doesn't feel rendered flat. Skip if it causes perf issues.

Dark mode skips the noise entirely; the deep midnight is already textured by the warm tone.

## Page-level moves

- **Home**: bigger optical title in Fraunces italic. Subtitle in Geist regular. Stage rows get the tiny glyph + warmer spacing. Per-stage progress shown as a thin warm bar that fills with the accent colour.
- **Lessons index**: cards with format-badge colour (cultural-deep-dive = accent red, concept-across-cultures = secondary gold). Card hover lifts 2px and slightly warms the background.
- **Toy page**: notebook-paper feel — soft horizontal rules between sections, slightly off-axis subtitle. Code block stays high-contrast against the cream.
- **Lesson-set entry**: a record-sleeve-style hero with the lesson title in Fraunces italic at large optical size.
- **Review page**: empty state shows the mascot napping with the caption "the dancers are resting".

## What stays

- Skip-to-content link.
- Reduce-motion media query.
- Single nav (DropdownNav). No second horizontal nav.
- Bottom playground bar at the bottom of every page.
- Plain-language copy. Plain. No purple prose because the design is warmer.
