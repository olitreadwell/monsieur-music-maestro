# Practice log template

Copy this after each session. It keeps the habit visible and shows progress.

## How to use

1. Copy the block below into a new file: `art/music/log/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`
   (for example `art/music/log/2026-06-08-first-kick.md`).
2. Fill it in. Keep it short. One or two words per line is fine.
3. Never edit an old log. Always make a new one. (Git is the memory.)

The frontmatter changes from `template` to `log` in the copy, because a filled-in
log is a dated event.

## The block to copy

```markdown
---
domain: art
matter: log
energy: doing
time: 2026-06-08
privacy: soft
status: active
related:
  - ../learning-journey.md
---

# Session: <short title>

- **Stage:** (which stage today, e.g. Stage 1 - the pulse)
- **Goal (one small sentence):**
- **What I did:**
- **One thing that worked:**
- **One thing to fix next time:**
- **Did I move / dance to it?** (yes / no)
- **How it felt** (optional):
- **Next review date** (spacing: +1, then +3, +7, +14, +30 days):
```

## Why these fields

- **Goal / what happened / fix** is the learning loop: plan, do, reflect. This is the
  best-evidenced habit for learning on your own.
- **Next review date** is spaced practice. Come back to an old patch on the schedule
  and rebuild it from memory. That is what makes it stick.
- **Did I move?** because dancing to your beat is part of the method, not a bonus.
