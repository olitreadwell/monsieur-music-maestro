## What you're listening for

Close your eyes for 10 seconds. What can you hear right now? A fridge hum, a bird, traffic, your own breath. All of those are sounds.

Music is sound someone arranged on purpose.

The arrangement is the whole thing. Two notes played in the wrong order are not a song. The same two notes in the right order can stop a crowd. You are going to spend time on this site learning to hear the arrangement, not just the sound.

## How to read the code

We are not using code yet. Listening first.

## Try it

Find any song you love. Play 30 seconds of it.

Then press pause and sit in silence for 30 seconds.

Notice the difference: the song is arrangement. The silence is the absence of arrangement. Both are part of music: silence is not nothing. It is the space that gives sounds their shape.

## In the world

Every culture organises sound. Javanese gamelan, Indian raga, West African drumming, Andean pan pipes, Japanese koto, gospel choir, house music: these are all different answers to the same question: how do you arrange sound in time so it means something to a human body?

You will meet many of these answers on this site. None is more correct than the others. Each grew from a particular place, time, and community of people.

## Music theory note

Two words worth holding onto:

**Timbre** (pronounced "TAM-ber"): the colour of a sound. A violin and a trumpet playing the same note sound different. That difference is timbre. It comes from the physical shape of the instrument and how it vibrates.

**Silence**: an absence of sound that is still part of music. Composers and producers use silence the same way they use notes: to create tension, relief, space, and surprise.

## History

The oldest known musical instrument is a bone flute found in Hohle Fels cave in southern Germany. It was carved from the wing bone of a griffon vulture roughly 40,000 years ago. Humans have been arranging sound for an extraordinarily long time.

That flute-maker lived before writing, before cities, before agriculture. Whatever music meant to them, it mattered enough to spend hours carving a precise instrument from bone.

## Dance and body

Sit still and close your eyes for 30 seconds. Let some music play, or just sit in silence.

Notice what your body wants to do. It usually wants to move. A foot starts tapping. A head starts nodding. You did not decide to do that.

That involuntary response is the starting point of everything on this site. Your body already knows rhythm. You are just going to learn to name what it knows.
