## What this is

Press play, make noise. Prove it works. No pressure, no theory.

## Break it

- Change `bd` to `cp`. Replay. Different sound, same shape.
- Add a fifth slot: `s("bd hh sd hh cp")`. Hear it crowd in.
- Stop everything with Cmd+. (or Ctrl+.).

## Listen for

Any track you already love. Tap along with one finger. That tap is the same idea as your `bd`.

## History

Drum machines like the Roland TR-808 and TR-909 gave musicians their first programmable beats in the 1980s. Hip-hop, house, and techno were all shaped by their sounds. The names `bd`, `sd`, `hh`, `cp` come straight from those machines.

## Self-test

Can you make it play, change one sound, and stop it? No looking up the shortcut.
